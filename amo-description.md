## AMO listing description

### Summary (short, ~250 char limit)

Pre-select your poll answer during live PW.live classes and have it submitted
instantly the moment the poll opens — detected in real time, not on a delay.
Unofficial, built for personal use.

### Full description

Poll Assist for PW Live adds a small control next to the poll icon during
live PW.live classes. Pre-select the answer you've already worked out
(A/B/C/D), and the moment a poll appears on screen, it's selected — and
submitted, if you want — automatically.

This isn't for skipping problems. It's for the case where you've already
solved it yourself and just don't want to lose points to lag between the
poll opening and you noticing it.

Features:
• Detects polls in real time as they open, instead of checking on a timer
• Auto-submits your pre-armed answer, with an adjustable delay (0–5s)
• Settings popup for auto-submit, delay, auto-hide, and debug logging
• Works entirely locally — no accounts, no servers, no data collection

Privacy:
This extension makes no network requests of its own and sends nothing to
any server. Settings are stored only in your browser (never synced to an
account or the cloud). It requests a single permission — storage — and
nothing else.

Scope:
Works on live classes only. Recorded classes aren't supported.

This is an unofficial, independently built extension and isn't affiliated
with, endorsed by, or connected to Physics Wallah or PW.live in any way.
It may stop working if PW.live changes their site.
